Frozen River
Recorded: 2026-10-10

Biome ID: minecraft:frozen_river
Region: Frozen Wastes
Status: No longer used. It was the vanilla placeholder in Frozen Wastes pool slot 4 until 2026-10-17, when Howling Canyon replaced it (user's choice; see docs/biomes/howling_canyon.txt).

Note
Selecting this biome does not carve a river.

Former behavior
- Formed exactly one district in each region of its pool (BiomeDistricts).
- Surface rules, vegetation, mobs, structures and carvers are vanilla.
- Terrain is the shared region interior (about Y=75-85); the biome does not shape it.

Replacement
Howling Canyon (cronusfantasyterrain:howling_canyon) takes over the slot; its main canyon carries its own frozen river.
