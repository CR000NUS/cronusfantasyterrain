Region Border Mountains
Recorded: 2026-10-12
Updated: 2026-10-14

Biome ID: cronusfantasyterrain:region_border
Region: Shared mountain belts between all regions.
Status: Implemented custom biome. Replaced the former minecraft:jagged_peaks placeholder on 2026-10-12.

Selection
Used wherever the border mask (erosion channel) exceeds 0.12. Positions with a weaker nonzero mask take the neighboring region's biome.

Terrain (frozen; see docs/extra/project_notes.txt)
The shape comes from RegionLayout, not from this biome: connected crests, branching ridges, steep flanks and low gullies.
Belts extend up to 360 blocks either side of a region edge before coordinate warping, spanning about Y=28 to Y=305. Gullies below Y=63 fill with water.
Mountain columns are solid below their surface; there are no floating slabs or overhangs.

Materials (user drawing, 2026-10-14)
- Exposed faces below Y=220: blackstone.
- Exposed faces at and above Y=220: continuous snow cap, including steep faces, with no holes or scattered patches.
- No exterior deepslate, stone, tuff or ore.
- Buried rock and cave walls: deepslate with about 4% tuff patches, plus deepslate ores.
- Bedrock, cave air and fluids are preserved.

Climate
Temperature 0.8, downfall 0.3, precipitation on. No freeze_top_layer feature, so lower slopes are not blanketed in snow.

Features
Only the custom ore placements under cronusfantasyterrain:border/ore_*. They keep vanilla frequencies and height ranges, use deepslate ore variants, replace only deepslate/tuff and are discarded when exposed to air.
No dirt, gravel, granite, diorite, andesite, sediment disks, lakes, springs, geodes, dungeons or vegetation. The borderFeatureTest Gradle task enforces this whitelist.

Implementation
- BorderMaterialRule / BorderPalette choose the material. The border_exterior_floor density output (lowest neighboring surface) separates outdoor faces from buried rock; it does not change terrain.
- BorderChunkMaterials repaints newly generated chunks after neighboring features have spilled in. It keeps cave air, fluids and approved ores, and never rewrites old saved chunks.
