Region Border Mountains
Recorded: 2026-10-12
Updated: 2026-10-15

Biome ID: cronusfantasyterrain:region_border
Region: Shared mountain belts between all regions.
Status: Implemented custom biome. Replaced the former minecraft:jagged_peaks placeholder on 2026-10-12.

Selection
Used wherever the border mask (erosion channel) exceeds 0.12. Positions with a weaker nonzero mask (the foothills) take the neighboring region's biome.
Border materials follow the same 0.12 test per column, not the per-block biome, so mountain columns never contain region stone.

Terrain (frozen; changed on request 2026-10-15; see docs/extra/project_notes.txt)
The shape comes from RegionLayout, not from this biome: one giant mountain range with many broad, rounded peaks. Most of the massif lies between Y=175 and Y=250; rare summits reach about Y=300 and rare deep valleys reach down to about Y=150.
The mountains fill the space between the regions (rounded hexagons, about 1,700 blocks from centre to side) in fairly even belts of about 400 blocks foot to foot, including where three regions meet. The whole mountain front pushes up to 180 blocks into the regions in broad lobes and bays (decision 12, 2026-10-16). A short, steep ramp (about blocks 68–169 from the belt's outer edge, starting at the foothill rim) climbs from region ground onto the massif.
No holes, lakes, canyons or cliffs at or below region ground. The ravine (minecraft:canyon) carver was removed; cave carvers remain.
Mountain columns are solid below their surface; there are no floating slabs or overhangs.

Materials (user drawing 2026-10-14; blackstone replaced by deepslate 2026-10-15)
- Exposed faces: deepslate (plain, no tuff) below the snow.
- Snow (changed 2026-10-15): snow fades in through a patchy band around a wandering snowline (average Y=214, band 36 blocks tall, locally about ±17 blocks; BorderPalette.SNOW_LINE/SNOW_BAND; extended downward 2026-10-16). No snow below Y=179; from Y=264 every exposed face is snow. Ledges and gentle slopes catch snow first; steep faces stay bare deepslate longer.
- No exterior stone, blackstone, tuff or ore.
- Buried rock and cave walls: deepslate with about 4% tuff patches, plus deepslate ores.
- Bedrock, cave air and fluids are preserved.

Deepslate basin (user sketch, 2026-10-15)
Regions are "cauldrons": grass, dirt and stone sit in a deepslate bowl whose walls rise into these mountains.
Foothills keep vanilla surface and stone; deepslate starts below them at a basin floor that meets the surface at the mountain foot and curves down to Y=0 within 384 blocks.
Implemented by BasinMaterialRule, material_rule/region_basin.json and density_function/region_basin_floor.json (material channel only).

Climate
Temperature 0.8, downfall 0.3, precipitation on. No freeze_top_layer feature, so lower slopes are not blanketed in snow.

Features
Only the custom ore placements under cronusfantasyterrain:border/ore_*. They keep vanilla frequencies and height ranges, use deepslate ore variants, replace only deepslate/tuff and are discarded when exposed to air.
No dirt, gravel, granite, diorite, andesite, sediment disks, lakes, springs, geodes, dungeons or vegetation. The borderFeatureTest Gradle task enforces this whitelist.

Implementation
- BorderMaterialRule / BorderPalette choose the material. The border_exterior_floor density output (lowest neighboring surface) separates outdoor faces from buried rock; it does not change terrain.
- BorderChunkMaterials repaints newly generated chunks after neighboring features have spilled in. It keeps cave air, fluids and approved ores, and never rewrites old saved chunks.
