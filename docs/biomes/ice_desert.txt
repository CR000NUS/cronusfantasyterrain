Ice Desert
Recorded: 2026-10-10

Biome ID: None (not a registered biome).
Region: Frozen Wastes (by association).
Status: Earlier terrain prototype from the Test World preset. Not part of Fantasy Regions.

What exists
- test_terrain.json uses ice_desert_terrain where frozen_wastes_region noise is at least -0.1.
- ice_desert_terrain.json is a vertical density gradient from Y=58 to Y=98 (zero crossing at Y=78) plus ice_desert_relief noise scaled by 0.25 for gentle relief.
- ice_desert_relief.json uses base octave -8 and three octaves.
- The Test World uses minecraft:plains everywhere, so this prototype has no icy surface, weather or vegetation.

Still to design
Whether this becomes a custom biome; if so, its ID, ice/snow materials, vegetation, structures and mobs.
