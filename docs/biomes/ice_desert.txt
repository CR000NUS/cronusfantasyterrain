Ice Desert
Recorded: 2026-10-10
Updated: 2026-10-26

Biome ID: None (never a registered biome).
Region: Frozen Wastes (by association).
Status: Removed. It was a terrain prototype in the earlier Test World preset, which was removed together with its data files on 2026-10-16 (update_03.01.01). Never part of Fantasy Regions; kept here only as a design record.

What it was
- test_terrain.json used ice_desert_terrain where frozen_wastes_region noise was at least -0.1.
- ice_desert_terrain.json was a vertical density gradient from Y=58 to Y=98 (zero crossing at Y=78) plus ice_desert_relief noise scaled by 0.25 for gentle relief.
- ice_desert_relief.json used base octave -8 and three octaves.
- The Test World used minecraft:plains everywhere, so the prototype had no icy surface, weather or vegetation.

Still to design
Whether the idea returns as a custom biome; if so, its ID, terrain, ice/snow materials, vegetation, structures and mobs.
