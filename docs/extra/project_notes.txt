30. Columnar cliff faces in Howling Canyon (2026-10-17)
The user liked the massif layout ("The biome looks good. Don't change the terrain itself") and asked only for different cliff faces: not flat, straight, sheer walls but walls with steps (screenshots).
- First attempt rejected: random one-block ice columns of different heights at the foot of every cliff. "I ASKED TO SIMPLY HAVE THE WALLS HAVE SOME STEPS IN THEM LIKE 2 OR 3! NOT BE STRAIGHT FUCKING FLAT OR JAGGED AND UNPASSABLE! ... MAKE THE WALLS FLAT BUT ADD SIMPLE DAMN STEPS!"
- Final: every tier cliff (the first one up from the ground included) is a flat wall in 2 or 3 even steps (fixed per massif and tier), sheer pieces of wall with ledges about 1-2 blocks deep between them. The fine 4-block jitter of the cliff lines was removed so walls and ledges stay flat and even. Massif layout, tiers, gaps, river and materials unchanged.
- Never add random or jagged detail to the walls.
- Second report the same day: "LITERALLY FUCKING NOTHING HAS CHANGED FROM THE FLAT WALLS BEFORE! FIND OUT WHAT CAUSES THESE FLAT WALLS AND FIX THEM" (screenshot: two tall, flat, sheer walls along the river). Cause, measured by scanning the terrain for drops over 20 blocks (813 found): not the tier cliffs but (a) saddles switched on with a hard edge, ground on one side and the full saddle height (up to about 55 blocks) on the other, cut off straight along the river's ground strip; (b) the gap between massifs was measured only against the second-nearest massif centre, so where that switched the field jumped by up to 60 blocks; (c) saddle passes depended on which massif owned the column. Fix: saddles rise from their edge in the same stepped cliffs; gaps are measured against every neighbour; each saddle depends only on the two massifs it joins; each cliff gets 25% of its tier band for its steps so ledges stay at least about a block deep. Result: no drop over 20 blocks anywhere (howlingCanyonTest checks it). Massif layout unchanged apart from slightly smaller massifs where three meet (the old measurement overstated their room).

31. Natural, irregular terrain shapes (2026-10-27)
The user said: "Whenever i want terrain to be generated, please do NOT make any terrain symetrical or go mathmatical looking lines. For example when i say i want a hill, please make these hills "random" looking in terms of blocks like do not make hill tops a perfect circle or a perfect square or anything but as realistic as possible looking."
- Applies to all terrain generated from now on, in every biome and for every landform the user asks for (hills, mountains, massifs, plateaus, cliffs, valleys, canyons, rivers, piles, plates and so on).
- No symmetrical or geometric-looking shapes: no perfect circles, squares, ellipses or regular polygons (outlines, tops, plateaus or rings), no ruler-straight lines or edges, no concentric rings at even spacing, no features repeated at an even spacing or in a visible grid.
- Make every landform look as realistic as possible instead: irregular outlines that vary in radius with direction and at several scales, tops and peaks off-centre, slopes of different steepness on different sides, sizes, heights and spacing varied from one landform to the next, nothing a mirror or rotated copy of its neighbour.
- "Random" means natural and irregular, not noisy or jagged: it does not undo decisions 23 and 30. Where the user asks for something plain (flat walls with 2-3 even steps, plain ground between plates), keep it plain and simple, but its outline and its course across the land still follow natural, irregular lines.
- This is a rule for new work and for changes the user asks for. It does not by itself authorize changes to frozen terrain (borders, decision 1; Everwinter, decision 26).

32. Howling Canyon overhauled to natural shapes (2026-10-30)
Howling Canyon (decisions 27-30) was built before decision 31. Asked whether to leave it or bring it in line, the user said: "Please overhaul the entire Howling Canyon biome with the new 'natural' rule." Also: "Don't commit Howling Canyon yet."

- Saddles only join massifs that face each other (no free-standing ridges across empty cells). The fine 10-block wobble of the cliff lines was dropped: it frayed the cliffs along narrow ridge crests.
- River: bends of uneven length and swing plus a slow drift (no sine wave).
- howlingCanyonTest also checks the natural shapes now (off-centre tops, outlines varying with direction, varied sizes and spacing, uneven river bends) besides the old checks (no drop over 20 blocks, gaps, open river, materials).
