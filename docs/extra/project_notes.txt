STANDING DECISIONS (in force)

1. Border terrain is frozen (2026-10-10)
The user said: "The terrain of the border is fine now. Do NOT change it from now on until i specifically say otherwise. If you have to change it for a reason, then first tell me so before actually changing it."
This covers the border mountain shape: RegionLayout terrain, border width, region partition and terrain density outputs. Material-only changes need their own explicit authorization.
Revised on request 2026-10-15 (decisions 6 and 7); the freeze applies to the revised shape.

2. Border materials (2026-10-14 drawing, updated 2026-10-15)
- Exposed faces: deepslate (plain, no tuff) below the snow.
- Snow: natural, patchy transition around an average snowline of Y=214 (changed 2026-10-15, decision 7; band extended downward 2026-10-16: first patches from Y=179, full cap still from Y=264); no hard cut.
- No exterior stone, blackstone, tuff, ore or random patches.
- Underground: deepslate, about 4% tuff patches and deepslate ores.
- Digging into the mountains or their caves must reveal only deepslate, never stone.

5. Region basin / "cauldron" (2026-10-15, user sketch)
The user said: "Think of it like a of the regions like cauldrons. The deepslate (from underneath the stone layers of the overworld) all the way up the border mountains are the cauldron itself. The biome, the dirt floor, the stone underneath, all of that is the water inside the cauldron."
- Region interiors and foothills keep grass, dirt and stone when dug into.
- Below them a deepslate bowl rises from the vanilla deepslate line (Y=0) to the mountain foot and continues as the border mountains.

3. Border features
Only custom deepslate ore placements. No dirt, gravel, granite, diorite, andesite, vegetation or other decorative features.

4. Scorched Lands naming (2026-10-12)
Ember Lands was renamed Scorched Lands in documentation only. The code/data key stays ember_lands.

6. Higher border massif with a harsh transition (2026-10-15)
The user said: "Change the terrain of the border a bit more so that it's overall more leaning towards a higher altitude. There shouldn't be holes, lakes or cliffs at ground level or below anymore. The transitions from the region to the border should also be less slight and more harsh. It should still be a transition but a more harsh and quick transition."
- Massif floor Y=125, crests about Y=300; no belt terrain below region ground (Y=75).
- Ramp from region ground onto the massif within about 100 blocks; steep, but no sheer walls at ground level.
- Ravine carver removed from the border biome. Border width and region partition unchanged.

7. Broad mountain range and natural snowline (2026-10-15)
The user said the snow "starts with a hard cut, starting at a set world height. Change this so that snow has a natural looking transition."
And: "The border should look like 1 giant mountain range with many moutain peaks and rarely a deep cut. To put it into perspective, the majority of the border should be at a height between 70-100 blocks. A small amount has a chance to generate a mountain that reaches a height of 120 and a small amount has a chance to generate a valley that reaches a height of 60. It should look natural and realistic."
- The 60/70/100/120 figures were read as proportions of the range, with 120 at the previous highest crests (about Y=300): main band Y=175-250, rare summits to about Y=300, rare valleys to about Y=150.
- Smooth noise replaces the ridged multifractal, so mountains between valleys are broad, not thin and pointy.
- Ramp, border width and region partition unchanged.

8. Round regions with jagged rims (2026-10-15)
The user showed a sharp crease where two ramp walls met at a region corner and a long, uniform straight wall, and said: "So the regions should be more circular and the edges should be slightly more jagged. Since it's now a bit more circular shape, i want the radius of the region to be roughly somewhere between 1300-1500 blocks"
Sketches: one round region with a jagged rim; several irregular round regions with mountain gaps between them.
- Regions are round blobs, radius 1300-1500 blocks from centre to mountain foot, rim wobble up to about 60 blocks.
- Centres on a jittered hexagonal grid 3,800 blocks apart; mountains fill the gaps. Ownership still changes inside full mountains.
- Mountain shape, ramp, materials and snow unchanged.

9. Bigger regions, thinner borders, slightly sharper mountains (2026-10-15)
The user said: "Change the spacing a bit by making the regions slightly bigger and the borders a bit thinner." and "The mountains are a bit too smooth. Make them slightly more pointy/jagged again but not as much as it originally used to be."
- Region radius 1450-1650 blocks (was 1300-1500); spacing stays 3,800. Narrowest belt about 400 blocks foot to foot (was about 560). Ownership now changes halfway between two mountain feet.
- Moderate ridged crests added on top of the rounded peaks (RIDGE_STRENGTH = 55). Height bands unchanged: about 80% between Y=175 and Y=250.

10. Slightly less circular regions (2026-10-15)
The user said: "Make the regions slightly less circular."
- Per-region three-lobed shape (up to 8% of the radius, lobes pointing into the wide three-region mountain areas) and slight oval stretch (up to 6%), random strength and orientation.
- Radii are capped per direction, so the narrowest belt stays about 400 blocks. Median radius about 1495.

11. Rounded-hexagon regions, narrower borders (2026-10-15)
The user said: "Make the regions less circular so that the borders are not as wide anymore."
- Regions are rounded hexagons (HEX = 12) filling their share of the grid; the rim stays 200 blocks behind the halfway line to each neighbour in every direction. Belts about 400 blocks foot to foot everywhere, no wide three-region mountain areas.
- Per-region lobes and oval stretch reduced to 2% each (they cost space). Regions cover about 60% of the world (was about 50%).

12. Mountain foot reaching into the regions (2026-10-16)
The user asked for the rim of the border to "reach more randomly into the region itself" and drew a winding foot line with rounded tongues and bays.
- First attempt (low deepslate aprons in front of the ramp) rejected the same day: "too random", the gentle aprons did not blend in and ended at a sudden steep wall.
- Final: the whole mountain front (ramp and massif) pushes up to RIM_REACH = 180 blocks into a region in broad, smooth lobes and bays a few hundred blocks across, everywhere along the rim. No aprons; the ramp keeps its normal steep profile. Reach is one-sided, so no belt gets thinner, and it never forms detached islands.
- Region interiors (no border influence at all) shrink from about 60% to about 53% of the world.

PROJECT STATUS

- Eleven regions confirmed. Five are generated with vanilla placeholder pools: Frozen Wastes, Cold Meadows, Scorched Lands, Verdant Valley, Crystal Peaks. Six are planned with no design yet: Blooming Highlands, Dry Dunes, Enchanted Groves, Land of the Giants, Misty Marshes, Shadow Wilds.
- Twenty vanilla placeholder biomes (four per region) plus one custom biome, Region Border.
- Frostscar is a planned custom biome for Frozen Wastes. Ice Desert is an earlier Test World prototype, not a biome.
- Not implemented: custom biome terrain, custom cave biomes, mountain passes, oceans or coastlines. The Nether and End are vanilla.
- Targets Minecraft 26.3, NeoForge 26.3.0.43-beta, Java 25.
