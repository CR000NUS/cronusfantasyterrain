Frozen Wastes
Recorded: 2026-10-10
Updated: 2026-10-16

Status: Implemented theme; one custom biome (Everwinter) plus vanilla placeholders.
Preset key: frozen_wastes
Region index: 0

Theme
Frozen landscape. FrozenLand.java declares NAME = "Frozen Wastes" and REGION_INDEX = 0.

Biome pool (five biomes; each forms exactly one district per region)
- minecraft:snowy_plains (placeholder)
- minecraft:ice_spikes (placeholder)
- cronusfantasyterrain:everwinter (custom, slot 2; see docs/biomes/everwinter.txt)
- minecraft:snowy_taiga (placeholder)
- minecraft:frozen_river (placeholder)

Implemented custom biomes
- Everwinter (2026-10-16, made rougher the same day): rolling snow ground with frequent snow piles and rarer big snow/ice hills, a few ice cliffs and deep crevasses, fields of jumbled serac blocks split by cracks, rare ice spires, occasional deepslate outcrops; glacier ice under the snow, stone/deepslate below.

Planned custom biomes
- Frostscar: large height differences with valleys, canyons, cliffs and steep mountains; mostly deepslate under snow, with ice-and-snow glaciers. Design only; see docs/biomes/frostscar.txt.

Earlier concepts
- Ice Desert: Test World terrain prototype, not a biome. See docs/biomes/ice_desert.txt.

Current generation
Standard region behavior (see docs/regions.md): seeded rounded-hexagon regions about 3,400 blocks across, shared Region Border mountains at region edges. Everwinter adds its own terrain and materials; the placeholder biomes keep the plain interior around Y=75-85.

Still to design
The remaining custom biomes (replacing the placeholders), vegetation, structures and mobs.

Border mountains are shared by all regions and frozen; see docs/extra/project_notes.txt.
